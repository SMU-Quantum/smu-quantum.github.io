Quantum computing for decision-making

SMU Quantum is a research group in the School of Computing and Information Systems, led by Professor Hoong Chuin Lau. We study how to plan for supply disruptions, stock shortages and other risks, combining quantum computing, AI and operations research, and we test our methods against strong classical baselines.

Evidence from supply chains, disaster bonds and inventory planning
