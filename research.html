Research

Risk and resilience

We model how disruptions interact and evaluate responses such as changing inventory levels, diversifying routes and expanding storage. Our compound-disruptions report examines how to assess rare events in semiconductor supply chains, disaster bonds and inventory planning.

Compound-disruptions whitepaper · Maritime energy security paper

Adaptive optimisation

We use learning to adapt penalty parameters, decompose large problems and decide how to allocate work between quantum and classical solvers. For example, AutoQResearch tests whether a language model can improve a solver’s configuration on graph and vehicle-routing problems.

Beyond QAOA survey · AutoQResearch paper

Quantum hardware and benchmarking

We study how noise, compilation and qubit limits affect the solutions we get from real devices. Our hardware benchmark compares several methods on IBM Heron processors and tracks how circuit size and noise affect performance.

Hardware benchmarking paper · Benchmark instances
